# Spots Vision

## Mission

Help skateboarders discover, share and preserve the best skate spots around the world.

## Vision

Create the world's most complete map of skate culture, connecting skateboarders with places, stories and communities.

## Product Philosophy

The map is the protagonist.

Spots is not only about finding places.
It is about discovering the history and culture behind them.

## Core Principles

- Simple first experience.
- Map-first design.
- Community-driven content.
- Preserve skate history.
- Respect local skate culture.

## What Spots is NOT

- A generic social network.
- A replacement for skate communities.
- A platform focused only on popularity.

The goal is discovery, documentation and connection.
